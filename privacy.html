Privacy Policy

Productivity++ · Last updated November 3, 2026

Productivity++ is a personal, open-source productivity app (tasks, calendar, Eisenhower matrix and habit tracker). It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share anyone's data.

What data the app stores

  • Your tasks, habits and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Optional sync: if you connect a sync server, a copy of your tasks and habits is stored there so your devices stay in sync. The sync server is a Cloudflare Worker that you create and control in your own Cloudflare account. It's protected by a secret token that only you know.
  • Push notifications: if you turn them on, your browser's push subscription (an address the browser gives the app for delivering notifications) is stored on your sync server. It's used only to send you your own reminders.

Google Calendar data

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app asks for permission to:

  • See your list of calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly), so you can choose which calendars to show.
  • View and edit events (calendar.events), to show your events in the app and to create, update or delete events for tasks you mark "Add to Google Calendar".

How Google data is handled:

  • Events are fetched only to display them to you in the app. They're cached on your device so they're visible offline.
  • The app only changes or deletes events that it created for your tasks. It never changes other events.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on your own sync server (your Cloudflare Worker). They're never stored in the app's code or on any third-party server.
  • Google data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect. This revokes the app's access. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Productivity++'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. GitHub may log basic request data, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare Workers runs the optional sync server, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Browser push services (Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your browser) deliver notifications. The app's push messages carry no content. Your device fetches the reminder text directly from your sync server.
  • Open-Meteo provides the optional Dashboard weather. If you pick a place, its coordinates (or a city name you search for) are sent to Open-Meteo, under Open-Meteo's terms. Nothing else is sent.

Deleting your data

  • On a device: Settings → Data → Erase this device, or clear the site's data in your browser.
  • On the sync server: delete the KV namespace or the worker in your Cloudflare account.
  • Google access: disconnect in Settings or at Google account permissions.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13.
